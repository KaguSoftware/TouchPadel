ONE APPLICATION, ROLE-BASED VIEWS
The desktop app is a single build. What a user sees is

WHY THE CAFE LIVES ON THE WEB AND NOT IN THE APP
A guest at a table will not install an application to order a coffee, and requiring one is the largest cause of failure in QR ordering.
Scanning the table code with the phone's own camera opens the menu in the browser, already bound to that table, with nothing
to install and no account to create. The app is reserved for padel, where a guest has reason to return and hold an account.
NOT INCLUDED IN THE STACK
RevenueCat is not used. It handles App Store and Play subscriptions, and both stores prohibit in-app purchase for real-world
goods and services — court time and food are paid for outside the app. Phase 1 is desk payment only, so no payment SDK of
any kind is integrated. Recorded here so it is not mistaken for an omission.

MODULE 7
DEGRADED MODE
What happens when the venue loses its connection: while the desk cannot reach the
server, the desk is the only thing allowed to write.
WHY A SINGLE WRITER, RATHER THAN SYNCING EVERYONE
A cafe order is an append-only fact — two never contend, and replaying them is safe. A court booking is not. If the desk sells
court 3 at 19:00 offline while the app sells the same slot, both guests have been told they have it, and no merge rule invented
afterwards makes that true. Rather than detect such conflicts and telephone a customer to cancel, the system removes the
possibility: during an outage only one channel writes.
INCLUDED
DETECTION
WHAT EACH CLIENT DOES
THE QUEUE
THE PROTECTED HORIZON
Blocking every guest booking during an outage
would stop a member booking next Saturday
from home because a router failed at the
venue. The lockout covers only what the desk
could plausibly sell offline — today and
tomorrow, configurable. Bookings beyond it
continue normally.
NOT INCLUDED
THE ONE HONEST LIMIT
There is a short window between the desk
losing its connection and the server noticing. A
booking made in those seconds can still
collide with one taken offline; the database
constraint catches it on replay and shows the
desk a conflict rather than an overwrite. A short
heartbeat keeps that window to seconds — not
to zero, and it is not claimed to be.
ACCEPTANCE
With the network disconnected the till keeps
taking orders and the kitchen screen keeps
receiving them; app and website refuse near-
term writes; and on reconnect every queued
item appears exactly once, with stock and
totals reconciling.

OPERATING IN
IRAQ
Three local conditions that change what the software has to do. Recorded here so
they are decided now rather than discovered during installation.
TAX — NO FISCAL DEVICE REQUIRED
Iraq does not operate a general VAT or sales tax system, and there is no requirement for a government-registered
fiscal cash register or certified receipt device of the kind found in Turkey or the Gulf. A sales tax of 10% applies to
services rendered by deluxe and first-class restaurants and hotels; whether Touch Cafe falls within that
classification is a determination for Touch's accountant, not for Kagu.
What we build: a configurable tax rate held per item group, applied at the till, shown separately on the bill and
reported at day close. Setting it to zero, to ten per cent, or to different rates by category is a configuration Touch
controls. No fiscal device integration and no e-invoicing submission is in scope.
CURRENCY
The system holds one trading currency, configured
at setup, in which all prices, bills, stock costs and
reports are expressed.
Touch must confirm before build starts whether the
venue prices in Iraqi dinar or in US dollars. If both
are taken at the counter in practice, that is a dual-
currency till — a second display currency, a fixed or
editable daily rate, change given in the other
currency, and both totals reconciled at day close.
Dual currency is not included in this scope and
would be quoted as an addition.
CONNECTIVITY AND POWER
Iraq's power and connectivity conditions are the
reason module 7 exists. The till keeps trading
through an outage, queueing locally and syncing on
reconnect, while the app and website are locked
out of near-term writes so nothing can be sold
twice.
Two things remain Touch's responsibility: a
business internet connection wired to the till, and a
UPS on the till, its printer, the router and the switch.
Most interruptions in practice are power rather than
network, and a UPS costs little against the trading
it protects. Full offline running from an on-premises
database is a later phase.
LANGUAGE
The phase delivers English and Arabic. If the venue is in the Kurdistan Region and Kurdish is expected by guests
or staff, that is a third locale and a change request — inexpensive once the framework exists, but it must be raised
before content is written rather than after.

It exists to absorb store review, including one
