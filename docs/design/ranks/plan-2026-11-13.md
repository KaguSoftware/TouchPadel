# Ranks: who gets to be on top (plan, 2026-11-13)

Majed's brief: "Just playing shouldn't make them top. It should be something balanced: tournaments,
recorded matches, coaches who evaluate. And it should give us something back: a boost, a share my
rank on Instagram button."

Status: plan only. Nothing is built or applied. The DECIDE items in §11 are open.

---

## 1. What is wrong with today's board (0364)

Today a win gives +2 and a loss gives +1, summed over the period. **Losing still earns points**, so
the person who plays most wins the board.

We ran a simulation at our real volume: Hussein's reports show 203–285 completed bookings a month
from June to September, about 250 games a month. It covered 200 players with real-world activity
(a few play a lot, most play 1–3 times a month) over one quarter, averaged over 40 runs:

| | Today (+2 win / +1 loss) | Proposed rating |
|---|---|---|
| How good the top 10 really are (skill percentile, 50% = average) | **49%** | **90%** |
| Games the top 10 played in the quarter | 34 | 11 |
| Board order vs how much you play (rank correlation) | **0.98** | −0.03 |
| Board order vs real skill | −0.01 | **0.92** |

In plain words: today's board is a list of who played most. The proposed one is a list of who
plays best, and playing more does not move you up.

One more thing the simulation showed. A pure win/loss rating where everyone **starts equal** barely
works here (skill correlation 0.08), because people mostly play friends at their own level, so
results never connect the groups. **Starting levels fix it.** Seeding from the level a player
already declares at sign-up lifts the correlation to 0.87, and coach or desk confirmed levels lift
it to 0.92–0.94. That is why coaches belong in this design: they set where a player starts, not
how many points they hold.

Script: kept out of the repo (scratchpad `sim.py` / `sim2.py`), easy to re-run.

## 2. The rule in one paragraph

Every player has a **Touch Level**, a number from 1.0 to 7.0, the scale padel players already know
from Playtomic. It starts from the level a coach, the desk or the player set. After every confirmed
game it moves by **who you beat, how strong they were and by how much**. Beat a stronger pair and
you climb. Beat a weaker pair and you get almost nothing. Lose to a weaker pair and you drop.
**The top board is ordered by Touch Level**, and you appear on it only once your level is
*reliable*: enough recent games against enough different people. Separately, each month runs a
**Season Race** where activity, tournaments and boosts count, but only your best results. The Race
gives everyone something to chase and gives the club its levers (off-peak boosts, paid events). It
never touches the Touch Level.

Two numbers, two jobs:

| | Touch Level (skill) | Season Race (activity) |
|---|---|---|
| Moved by | confirmed results against expectations | best 8 match results + best 2 tournament finishes this season |
| Playing more | does not raise it (only makes it more certain) | helps, up to the cap |
| Boosts, events, money | **never** | yes, this is where they live |
| Resets | never (inactivity only hides you) | every season (month or quarter, owner's setting) |
| Shown as | the **Top** board, your tier, your share card | the **Race** board, prizes |

This is how real systems separate the two: DUPR, UTR and Playtomic rate skill from expected
results. The FIP pro ranking counts only the best 22 results. Riot and Valorant keep a hidden skill
rating apart from the visible season rank.

## 3. Touch Level: how it moves

**Start (seed)**

| Source | Starting level | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Coach "Level check" (§5) or desk/manager confirmed level | the coach's value | 50% (Playtomic resets reliability to 50% the same way) |
| Self-declared at sign-up (`profiles.play_level`, required since 0368) | new 1.0 · beginner 2.0 · intermediate 3.2 · advanced 4.5 | low (provisional) |

**After each confirmed game** (team = average of the two players; Playtomic, DUPR and AFPadel all
do this):

- expected = 1 / (1 + 10^((their team − our team) / 1.0)). A one-level gap means the stronger pair
  is expected to win 91% of the time.
- actual = 0.7 × (win 1, loss 0) + 0.3 × (share of games won), when a score is entered. Win or
  loss alone when it is not. A 7-6, 7-6 loss to a much stronger pair can lift you a little (DUPR
  and UTR use points or games won the same way).
- change = K × trust × (actual − expected), the same for both partners.

| Setting | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| K, first 5 rated games or after a coach check | 0.5 | finds your level fast; also catches sandbaggers quickly |
| K, games 6–20 | 0.3 | settling |
| K, after 20 | 0.15 | stable; FIDE steps K down the same way |
| Trust: tournament or desk-entered result | ×1.0 | verified (AFPadel: tournament 100%, interclub 75%) |
| Trust: player-reported, opponent confirmed | ×0.75 | DUPR and UTR weight self-posted results lower |
| Same four players again within 30 days | 3rd–4th game ×0.5, 5th and later ×0 | stops farming with friends |
| Loss for a player under 2.5 | ×0.6 | AFPadel softens losses for weaker players; keeps beginners playing |
| Biggest move from one game | ±0.25 | nobody jumps a band on one lucky night |

**Inactivity:** the level never decays. With no rated game in 45 days you drop off the board but
keep your number. Your next 3 games count at K 0.3, because your level is less certain (Glicko's
idea).

**Voids and corrections:** the level is *derived*: replay every confirmed result in order. A void
or a corrected score just replays. At about 250 games a month a full replay takes milliseconds, and
it means no level ever drifts from the results behind it.

**Reliable** (the gate for the Top board): at least **6 rated games in the last 90 days**, against
at least **4 different opponents**, and past the first 5 provisional games. The simulation's median
player plays about 6 games a quarter, so roughly 60% of active players qualify in a quarter. DUPR
calls 60% reliability "reliable" and folds opponent variety into it. Lichess hides provisional
ratings from leaderboards.

## 4. The boards

The Ranks tab gets four views. The current public rule stays: the top N only, nobody below them is
ever fetched by anyone else.

1. **Top**: by Touch Level, reliable players only. Top 10 (the existing `board_size`).
2. **By class**: Advanced (A) · Intermediate (B) · Beginner (C), the same A/B/C as tournament
   classes (0359). A beginner can be #1 of C. Bands: C under 2.5, B 2.5–3.9, A 4.0 and up.
3. **Most improved this month**: biggest Touch Level gain, minimum 4 games. The board a beginner
   can win in their first month.
4. **Season Race** (§6).

**Tiers** keep today's five names and table (`ranks_levels`), but read the Touch Level instead of
lifetime points:

| Tier | Touch Level |
|---|---|
| Rookie · مبتدئ | under 2.0 |
| Rising · صاعد | 2.0 – 2.9 |
| Contender · منافس | 3.0 – 3.9 |
| Elite · نخبة | 4.0 – 4.9 |
| Legend · أسطورة | 5.0 and up |

**Your own page (Mine)** shows your level, your tier, reliability ("3 more games to qualify for the
board" when you are not reliable yet), your board rank or your **percentile** ("top 18% of
players"), and how far you are from the next tier. Studies of leaderboards in education find that
showing someone their own neighbourhood motivates every level. Showing the bottom of an absolute
list demotivates.

## 5. Coaches: they set the starting point, they never hand out points

Every real system found (Playtomic, DUPR, LTA, Nettla) uses the coach to **set or calibrate** a
level, never as an ongoing source of points. Here:

- **Level check**: a short paid session (a product the desk sells like a lesson; Playtomic sells
  exactly this as a "levelling session" and pitches it to clubs as revenue). The coach sets the
  level with the screens that already exist (0401 `coach_set_student_level` for their linked
  students; 0368 `staff_set_customer_level` for desk, manager and owner). New: the coach picks
  band + low/mid/high (12 steps) instead of just the band.
- **Effect**: Touch Level = the coach's value, confidence back to 50% (K 0.5 for the next 5
  games). Results then confirm or correct the coach.
- **Limits against favouritism**: once per player per 60 days. On a reliable player a coach may
  move the level by at most ±0.5. The coach's name is stamped (the audit `customer.level_confirmed`
  already exists).
- **Coach calibrations list** for the owner: each check, and where that player's level went in the
  20 games after it. A coach whose players always fall after the check is rating too high, and it
  shows.

This also replaces 0368's `level_suggestions` (average opponent level ± 0.5). The Touch Level band
*is* the suggestion, one truth instead of two.

## 6. Season Race

Each season (the existing `ranks_settings.period`: month or quarter) has its own board, per class,
from these points:

| Source | Points | Counted |
|---|---|---|
| Match win | 10 × (1 + how much stronger the other pair was, capped at ×2) | **best 8** results this season |
| Match loss | 2 | (part of the same best 8) |
| Tournament finish | winner 100 · final 60 · semi 36 · top half 18 · played 8 (FIP's ratios, scaled) | **best 2** tournaments |
| Boost (§7) | multiplies the result it is on | max 3 boosted results per season |

Best-N is how the pro tour stops sheer volume from winning. Playing 30 times gets you no further
than your best 8 nights.

**Prizes** at season end, top 3 of each class: a free court hour in an off-peak slot (which costs
us an empty court), a loyalty reward, and a badge on the share card. Cheap for us, and it is a
reason to play this month, not "some day".

## 7. Boosts: how the ranking pays the club back

Rule zero: **a boost never touches the Touch Level.** Studies of pay-to-win (Petrovskaya & Zendle
2021; Freeman et al., CHI PLAY 2022) show that paying for skill standing destroys trust in the
rank, and a rank nobody trusts is worth nothing to share. Boosts live in the Race only, are capped,
and carry a ⚡ next to the result so everyone can see them.

| Boost | What it does | What the club gets |
|---|---|---|
| **Off-peak boost** ×1.5 | Race points from games on bookings that start in hours the owner marks off-peak | fills empty courts. June had 251 not-completed slots against 285 played; September had 156 not booked. |
| **Event boost** ×2 | results in club events the owner flags: Americano nights, ranked tournaments with an entry fee | entry fees, full courts, café traffic around events |
| **Boost token** ×2 on one result | bought with **loyalty points**, not cash | gives loyalty points (earned by spending money) something exciting to buy; ties the café to the court |
| **Welcome boost** ×1.5 | a game where one of the four is playing their first rated game | rewards bringing new players in |

A cash-bought boost is DECIDE 4. Recommended: no. Playtomic Premium sells stats, history graphs and
promoted matches, never level points. If we want a paid tier later, sell those, not rank.

Other ways the ranking feeds the business without any boost:

- Rated results only come from **booked and played courts** (0364 already ties a player report to
  a reservation). Ranked play is booked play.
- **Coach Level checks** (§5) are a paid product, and "you're 0.3 from Elite" is the natural lead
  into lessons.
- **Tournament seeding and class checks** use the Touch Level when it is reliable, so tournaments
  get fairer brackets (today `tournament_register` checks the self-declared level).

## 8. Share my rank (Instagram story card)

**When the app offers it** (a moment worth showing off, never a nag): moving up a tier, entering a
top 10, a Race podium, a tournament win, and a season-end card ("Your November at Touch Padel":
games, wins, level change, best partner).

**The card**: 1080×1920 (Instagram's 9:16; the minimum is 720×1280). It shows the tier badge,
Touch Level, board rank and class, this season's record, a small level line over the season, the
club logo and handle, and a **QR code to the player's invite link**. The player chooses "First L."
or their full name.

**How it is made**: rendered **on the phone** from a normal React Native view and captured as an
image (`react-native-view-shot`; neither it nor `expo-sharing` is installed yet, so check both
against Expo SDK 58). On the phone, Arabic shapes correctly with the app's own fonts. A
server-side image renderer would risk broken Arabic.

**How it is shared**, in two steps:

1. **Share sheet** (`expo-sharing`): Instagram, WhatsApp, Snapchat and Telegram all appear. No Meta
   setup is needed. Ship this first.
2. **Direct "Add to Instagram Story" button**: Meta's Stories intent (iOS
   `instagram-stories://share` + pasteboard, Android `com.instagram.share.ADD_TO_STORY`). It needs
   a Facebook App ID (mandatory since January 2023), the iOS `LSApplicationQueriesSchemes` entry
   and a native share module. Meta does not document a way for an app to pre-fill a link sticker,
   so the QR and handle go in the image itself.

**The payback has to be measurable.** Instagram never tells us who shared, so **never reward the
share itself**. Reward what we can see: the QR opens `/{locale}/invite/{code}` on the club site
(the app store links with the code). When a new player signs up with that code **and plays their
first booked game**, the sharer gets loyalty points. Track share taps → invite opens → sign-ups →
first bookings. (Spotify Wrapped and Wordle show share cards spread; no hard referral numbers for
sports cards were found, so we measure our own.)

## 9. Anti-abuse

| Trick | What stops it |
|---|---|
| Inventing results with friends | an opponent must confirm (0364), and the result must sit on a booked, played court (0364). Player reports count ×0.75. Same four again: ×0.5 from the 3rd game, nothing from the 5th. |
| Beating beginners for points | expected-score maths: a pair a full level above wins almost nothing. |
| Trading wins back and forth | the same four with the winner flipped within 7 days lands on a **Results to check** list beside 0368's "Levels to check". A manager voids it (void exists) and the level replays. |
| Sandbagging (claiming low, then winning) | provisional K 0.5 moves them up in a handful of games. A coach check resets them. |
| Hiding on top | 45 days without a game drops you off the board. |
| A friendly coach | ±0.5 cap, 60-day limit, name stamped, calibrations list (§5). |
| Buying the top | impossible: money and boosts never reach the Touch Level. |

## 10. What it builds on, and what is new

Already there (0364, 0368, 0401, 0359): `game_results` (sides, winner, score, source, confirm,
void), `ranks_ledger`, `ranks_settings`, `ranks_levels`, the Ranks tab (Board + Mine + live
updates), the desk result entry, the coach level screen, tournament classes A/B/C and "Levels to
check".

New:

- **DB** (new migrations; check the directory for the next free ordinals at build time, because
  0412–0413 are claimed by the café-history session):
  - the replay function and a derived `player_ratings` cache (level, games, distinct opponents,
    reliable, last game);
  - `ranks_settings` gains the K values, trust weights, gates (6 / 4 / 90 / 45), best-N (8 / 2),
    off-peak windows and boost multipliers;
  - `ranks_levels.min_points` becomes a level threshold;
  - the Race reads the ledger with the new formula;
  - a boost-token table;
  - invite codes;
  - level checks become a coach product;
  - "Results to check" and "Coach calibrations" reads.
- **Mobile**: Top / Class / Improved / Race views; Mine with level, reliability and percentile; the
  report form nudges for a score; the share card screen; the boost picker.
- **Operator**: owner settings for the above, off-peak windows, an "event boost" switch on
  tournaments, the two review lists.
- **Switch-over**: replay every confirmed `game_results` row since 0364 to build day-one levels.
  Today's points become "Season 0" history. Announce Season 1's start date.

**Data I could not see:** hosted results (no service key in this session). Before launch, count the
confirmed hosted `game_results` and check how many players already have a confirmed level. That
decides how many players start with a real seed.

Suggested order: (1) Touch Level + reliable gate + Top/Class boards; (2) coach Level check +
calibration list; (3) Season Race + prizes + off-peak/event boosts; (4) share card with the share
sheet + invite codes; (5) boost tokens for loyalty points; (6) direct Instagram button.

## 11. DECIDE (Majed)

1. **Show the number?** Show "3.4" with one decimal (recommended; padel players know the scale), or
   only tier names and rank.
2. **Top board = Touch Level** (recommended) or the Race.
3. **Season length** for the Race: month (recommended, quick reward loop) or quarter.
4. **Boosts for cash?** Recommended: no; loyalty points only.
5. **Level check**: paid? price? Which coaches may run it?
6. **Coach scale**: band + low/mid/high (recommended) or any value in 0.5 steps.
7. **Neighbours on Mine**: percentile only, no names (recommended, keeps today's "nobody below the
   top is fetched" rule), or show the names just above and below you.
8. **Race prizes** per class: what, and how many.
9. **Share card name**: "First L." default, full name optional (recommended).
10. **Women's / mixed boards**: KNLTB flags mixed pairs as a padel-specific rating problem. One
    board for all, or a separate women's board?
