# Touch Shop as its own desk (2026-09-27)

Parsa, 2026-09-27: "the touch shop has its own desk. its own stock, its own items." The
2026-09-22 build (0143–0146) sold shop products from the café till, split out of the café
basket, with their stock in the café store. This replaces that with a separate desk. It
supersedes the "hybrid" row of the `PHASE-2-PLAN.md` decisions table and the
products/product_variants sketch at `PHASE-2-PLAN.md:371`.

## Decisions (Parsa)

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Who sells | A dedicated role, `shop_staff` ("Shop assistant"), who sees the shop and nothing of the café, the courts or the reports |
| Money | Its own drawer (the till shift at the shop PC) and its own figures at day close (a Shop block inside the one venue close) |
| Cross-sell | None: every shop sale is paid on the spot at the shop desk, never on a booking or a café bill |
| Station | Its own PC, registered with station mode `shop` |
| Shop staff on their own | Goods in, stock counts, add and edit shop products and their prices, small discounts at the cashier's limit |
| Refunds and voids | Manager PIN, as at the café |
| Receipts | Printed after every sale on the shop PC's printer, in the café receipt format |
| Court desk | Loses its shop stock view on the phone |

## The model

Shop products stay menu items in `menu_categories.kind = 'shop'` sections, each size a
variant with its own `retail` stock row (0143–0146). The whole sale path (tab, order, lines,
payments, refunds, reports, availability, the `shop_launch` approval, branch copying)
already keys on menu items; rebuilding it on new tables would have been weeks of work for the
same behaviour. The separation is done in the rules and the screens instead:

- **The tab kind decides who works it** (`app.assert_tab_kind_role`, 0244): `shop_staff`
  works shop tabs only, the cashier and the court desk café tabs only, management both.
  `TAB_KIND_FORBIDDEN` otherwise.
- **No mixing** (0244): a shop tab carries no table or booking (`SHOP_TAB_NO_ANCHOR`), a shop
  item never goes on a café tab nor a café item on a shop tab (`TAB_KIND_MISMATCH`), and two
  tabs of different kinds never merge.
- **The shop store** (0243, 0245): `stock_location` gains `shop`. Retail stock lives there and
  only there, and the shop store holds only retail (`app.assert_store_for_kind`). A shop sale
  draws from it, a return restocks it, nothing moves in or out of it, production never uses it.
- **Reads** (0244, 0245): new permissive policies give `shop_staff` the shop's tabs, orders,
  lines, payments, refunds and adjustments, and the shop store's ingredients, batches,
  movements, deliveries, counts and suppliers; the café's rows stay invisible to them.
- **Products and prices** (0246): `upsert_menu_item`, `upsert_variant` and
  `upsert_retail_variant` admit `shop_staff` for shop categories only. The manager-only price
  and launch protocols name the manager, so they do not reach the shop assistant.
  `app.upsert_shop_category` makes the shop's own sections.
- **Day close** (0246): `app.day_close_shop` returns the shop's sales by method, refunds, net,
  unpaid sales (which hold `close_day`) and the shop stations' till shifts.

## The apps

- **Operator.** A `shop` workspace (the shop assistant's only one; management opens it from
  the Shop row): `/shop` (the shop till: scan or tap, basket, Cash / Card, receipt),
  `/shop/drawer`, `/shop/stock`, `/shop/receive`, `/shop/counts`,
  `/shop/products`, `/shop/suppliers` (the shop's Waste page was removed on 2026-10-08; the
  café's `/stock/waste` stays). The stock pages are the café's own screens told to keep
  the shop store (`StockScopeProvider`). The café till, New tab, the menu editor and the café's
  stock pages no longer show shop items. Day close shows the Shop block. Settings › Stations
  and first-run setup offer the `shop` mode.
- **Phone.** The shop assistant's staff area has the shop stock view, adding stock and counting,
  all at the shop store. The court desk has no stock pages any more.
- **Web.** Unchanged: the guest menu already lists café sections only.

## Rollout

1. The migrations reach hosted with the operator build that uses them. They refuse the café
   cashier on shop tabs, so an older operator's counter sale stops working the moment 0244
   lands. Nobody sells from the shop yet (no products on hosted), so the window costs nothing.
2. The next operator tag is installed on every station, and the shop PC is registered with the
   `shop` mode in Settings › Stations.
3. Only then does the owner create shop assistant accounts (Staff › Add staff, role "Shop
   assistant"): an older operator reads an unknown role as no access.
4. The shop's sections and products are entered at `/shop/products`; stock arrives through
   `/shop/receive`.
