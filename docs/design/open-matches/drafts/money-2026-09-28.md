## 3. Money and tickets (lane Money)

This lane owns the ticket purchase, the ticket ledger's money edges (bought, cashed out, refunded), the
seat money at the desk, the write-offs and the online and ticket figures in reports and day close.
The DB lane owns the ticket moves inside a match (`ticket_lock`, `ticket_release`, `ticket_forfeit`,
§1.5); this section states the rules those moves must keep. Code was read on 2026-09-27 at `fa86fe09`
(latest migration 0248). `NNNN:line` is a line in `packages/db/supabase/migrations/2026…NNNN_*.sql`;
`fn/` is `packages/db/supabase/functions/`, `op/` is `apps/operator/src/`, `mob/` is `apps/mobile/src/`.

### 3.1 Where every dinar lives

| Money | Record | Counts as | Never counts in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A ticket purchase (Qi) | `booking_payments` row, `purpose='ticket'`, `venue_id` NULL, `ticket_count` 1..3 | a chain liability while its tickets are `available`/`reserved`/`in_use` | `court_fee_paid`, `deposit_net_paid`, `my_reservations`, `booking_bill`, cash/card, any branch figure |
| A forfeited ticket | `match_tickets.status='forfeited'`, `forfeited_venue_id` | revenue of the branch where the no-show happened, dated `forfeited_at` | court money |
| A cashed-out ticket | `match_tickets.status='cashed_out'` + the purchase row's single Qi refund | money returned | revenue |
| A seat's share paid at the desk | a settled booking tab (court line capped by `tabs.court_cap_iqd`), its `payments` row, `payment_match_seats` links | court money of the match booking (`court_fee_paid`, cash/card) | ticket figures |
| A share nobody pays (no-show, late leave not refilled, vacant seat after start, manager write-off) | derived, never stored as money: `app.court_fee_written_off` | written off | cash, card, revenue collected |
| A deposit (existing) | `booking_payments`, `purpose='deposit'` | unchanged from 0242 | ticket figures |

A ticket is **not** a prepayment of a share. It is a bond: a player who shows gets the ticket back and
pays the full `share_iqd` at the desk (OM-45); a no-show loses the ticket and the share is written off.

### 3.2 Defaults this lane takes (Parsa may reverse any)

| # | Default | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MD-1 | A ticket purchase row is a chain row: `booking_payments.venue_id` is NULL for `purpose='ticket'`. | Tickets are chain-wide (§1.2 `match_tickets` has no venue). Any branch stamp would be a guess (0125 R1) and would leak ticket money into that branch's figures. |
| MD-2 | A valid SUCCESS always creates `ticket_count` tickets, however late. The only refunds on a SUCCESS are `amount_mismatch` (not our payment, 0242:681-690) and `account_deleted` (nobody can use them). A second purchase that succeeds is a second purchase, never `duplicate_success`. | A ticket holds no slot, so nothing can be "lost" (the 0242 `slot_lost` case does not exist). |
| MD-3 | Cash-out marks the tickets `cashed_out` **when the refund is requested**, not when Qi confirms it. | The tickets must stop being usable the moment money is promised back; the refund request id is minted once (0242:191) and the refund machine (`refund_pending → refunded`, `refund_failed → retry | manual`) guarantees the money reaches the guest. Restoring tickets on a Qi failure would leave both a usable ticket and an owed refund. |
| MD-4 | Cash-out is per purchase, takes **all** of that purchase's `available` tickets in one Qi refund, and is refused (`TICKET_IN_USE`) while any ticket of that purchase is `reserved` or `in_use`. | A purchase row allows one refund (0241:56-57, 0242:179). Cashing out part of a purchase would make the rest unrefundable for ever. |
| MD-5 | A guest with a `match_ban` cannot buy tickets (`MATCH_BANNED`). | Tickets only buy match seats; selling one the buyer cannot use invites a cash-out. |
| MD-6 | At most `3 × platform_settings.max_filling_matches_per_guest` `available` tickets per guest after the purchase (default 9; `TICKET_COUNT_INVALID`, detail `wallet_limit`). | OM-20 (3 seats) × OM-37 (3 filling matches) is the most a guest can ever lock at once; more is only liability. |
| MD-7 | One live ticket attempt per guest; three failed or expired attempts in 24 hours → `TOO_MANY_ATTEMPTS`. The payment window is 900 s. | The deposit rules (0242:393-397, 0241:140) with no hold to count against. |
| MD-8 | Seat money is settled one fresh tab per desk action, in full. The desk never leaves a partly paid tab on a match booking. | B5 of the review: a partly paid tab blocks day close (0216:158-162) and cannot be cancelled (0244:621-635). |
| MD-9 | Unassigned court money on a match booking (paid on the normal bill, or linked to a seat that no longer carries a share) is applied automatically: to a price rise first, then to owing seats from the highest `seat_no` down, then to write-offs. | Keeps Σ seat owed = the booking's remaining, so the last payer is never refused and the day closes. `match_link_payment` makes it precise. |
| MD-10 | DF-16 is enforced in the operator UI, not by the server. | The seat money model ignores café lines (links are capped at the tab's `court_iqd`), so a café line on a match booking costs no correctness; a server refusal needs re-issuing `till_add_items` and `merge_tabs` (0244), the till's hottest paths. |
| MD-11 | A manager write-off is final in v1 (no undo RPC). | Rare; an undo would need its own PIN path. See open question 4. |

#### 3.5.1 `app.ticket_payment_prepare(p_guest_id uuid, p_count int, p_locale text, p_provider text) → jsonb`

Service role only (edge `ticket-begin`, on behalf of the JWT user). Refusals, in this order:

1. `INVALID_ARGUMENT`: `p_guest_id` null; `p_provider` not `qi`/`fake` (detail `p_provider`).
2. `ACCOUNT_REQUIRED`: no profile, or `deleted_at` set.
3. A live attempt of this guest (`purpose='ticket'`, `created|pending`, locked `for update`) → return it
   with `reused: true`, whatever `p_count` says (a double tap; the app shows that attempt's count).
4. `TICKET_COUNT_INVALID`: `p_count` null or not 1..3 (detail `p_count`).
5. `MATCHES_OFF`: no branch in `app.open_venue_ids()` (0225:25) has `venue_settings.matches_enabled`.
6. `PHONE_REQUIRED`: blank `profiles.phone` (as 0242:385-389; Qi's customer phone and OM-36).
7. `TERMS_REQUIRED`: the test `app.match_guest` applies (§2), evaluated for `p_guest_id`
   (`match_guest` reads `auth.uid()`, which is NULL under the service role, so the checks are inline; a
   test pins both refusal orders).
8. `MATCH_BANNED`: a `customer_flags` row `type='match_ban'` for the guest (MD-5).
9. `TICKET_COUNT_INVALID`, detail `wallet_limit`: `available` tickets + `p_count` > 3 ×
   `platform_settings.max_filling_matches_per_guest` (MD-6).
10. `TOO_MANY_ATTEMPTS`: ≥ 3 of the guest's ticket rows `failed|expired` with `created_at > now() − 24 h`.

Then: unit price `v_unit := platform_settings.match_ticket_price_iqd`; insert
`(venue_id NULL, reservation_id NULL, hold_id NULL, guest_id, purpose 'ticket', ticket_count p_count,
provider, sandbox = profiles.payment_sandbox, request_id gen_random_uuid(), amount_iqd v_unit × p_count,
quoted_price_iqd v_unit, locale, status 'created', deadline_at now() + 900 s)`. A `unique_violation` on
`booking_payments_one_active_ticket` (two taps at once) re-reads and returns the live row as `reused`.
Event `begin`; audit `ticket.purchase_begin`. No `assert_not_degraded_for`: a ticket holds no slot.

Returns `{id, request_id, purpose:'ticket', status, provider, sandbox, amount_iqd, ticket_count,
unit_price_iqd, deadline_at, form_url, provider_payment_id, locale, reused, guest_phone, guest_name}`.

#### 3.5.3 `deposit_apply` ticket branch (0255, re-issued from 0242:605) and `app.ticket_settle_success`

`deposit_apply`, after the row is matched (0242:630-637):
- **Locks.** A ticket row takes no court and no reservation lock: `select … from booking_payments where
  id = v.id for update` only, and no `set_config('app.venue_id')` (the venue is NULL). Deposit rows keep
  0242:639-644 verbatim.
- **SUCCESS**, status `created|pending|failed|expired`: the amount and currency test (0242:682-690) is
  shared; a mismatch is `amount_mismatch` (whole row, no tickets). Otherwise `v_new :=
  app.ticket_settle_success(v.id)` for a ticket row, `app.deposit_settle_success(v.id)` for a deposit.
- **FAILED / EXPIRED**: unchanged status writes; the hold release (0242:712-717) runs only for
  `purpose='deposit'`. No tickets, nothing to undo.
- The returned JSON gains `purpose` and `ticket_count`.

`app.ticket_settle_success(p_payment_id uuid) returns text`, internal, service path. The caller holds the
purchase row lock. It never raises on a valid row (a raise rolls back `deposit_apply`, the webhook
answers 500 and the reconciler loops, the M5 lesson of the review).
1. If `match_tickets` rows already exist for this purchase, return the row's status (replay).
2. `status='succeeded'`, `succeeded_at = coalesce(succeeded_at, now())`, `failure_code = null`.
3. Insert `ticket_count` tickets: `guest_id = bp.guest_id`, `status 'available'`,
   `price_iqd = bp.quoted_price_iqd`, `sandbox = bp.sandbox`, `purchase_payment_id = bp.id`; one
   `bought` event each with `payment_id`.
4. If `bp.guest_id` is NULL or the profile has `deleted_at`: `app.tickets_cash_out(bp.id,
   'account_deleted', null)` (§3.7.2) and return `refund_pending`.
5. Audit `ticket.purchase` `{payment_id, ticket_count, amount_iqd, sandbox}`; return `succeeded`.

No push on a purchase: the payment screen is open and shows the result.

`attempts_left > 0` (never on `holdLive`); `refund_*` with `amount_mismatch` → "Not charged, money on

### 3.6 The ticket ledger

| From → to | Who | Event | Money |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| (new) → `available` | `ticket_settle_success` | `bought` | liability + price |
| `available` → `reserved` | `match_request` (DB) | `reserved` | none |
| `reserved` → `in_use` | `match_decide` approve (DB) | `locked` | none |
| `reserved` → `available` | decline, withdraw, request expiry, match end (DB) | `released` | none |
| `available` → `in_use` | `match_start`, `match_join` (DB) | `locked` | none |
| `in_use` → `available` | leave while filling, bump, expiry, cancel, OM-44 removal, refill of a `left_late` seat, `attended`, call-off for a present player, staff cancel (DB) | `released` | none |
| `in_use` → `forfeited` | seat `no_show`; `left_late` seat at start with no refill; call-off for the no-show (DB) | `forfeited` | liability − price, forfeit revenue + price at `forfeited_venue_id` |
| `forfeited` → `available` or `in_use` | attendance correction `no_show` → `attended` inside the DB lane's window | `restored` | the forfeit reverses |
| `available` → `cashed_out` | `ticket_cashout`, `ticket_settle_success` (deleted), `ticket_refund_deleted` | `cashed_out` | liability − price, one Qi refund per purchase |

Rules the DB lane's `ticket_lock` / `ticket_release` / `ticket_forfeit` must keep:
- Pick the guest's `available` tickets whose `sandbox = matches.sandbox` (DF-19), oldest first
  (`created_at, id`); lock them `for update` in id order (§1.4). Friend seats use the holder's tickets.
- `ticket_forfeit` stamps `forfeited_at`, `forfeited_venue_id = matches.venue_id`, `forfeited_seat_id`;
  a restore clears all three.
- Never touch a `cashed_out` ticket, and never move a ticket out of `forfeited` except by `restored`.
- Write one `match_ticket_events` row per move, with `match_id`, `seat_id` or `request_id`, and
  `actor_staff_id` when staff caused it.

### 3.7 Cash-out, refund failures, DF-20, DF-21

#### 3.7.1 `app.ticket_cashout(p_customer_id uuid, p_purchase_payment_id uuid) → jsonb` (0255)

Refusals, in order: `FORBIDDEN`; `INVALID_ARGUMENT` (a null argument); `PAYMENT_NOT_FOUND` (not a ticket

Steps: lock the purchase's tickets `for update` in id order, then the purchase row `for update`. If the

Returns `{duplicate, payment_id, tickets_cashed_out, refund_amount_iqd, status:'refund_pending'}`.

#### 3.7.2 `app.tickets_cash_out(p_payment_id uuid, p_reason text, p_staff_id uuid) returns jsonb` (internal)

'N tickets')` (0242:165) and `app.deposit_nudge()`. The refund amount is the price **paid** (DF-21),
never today's price.

#### 3.7.3 Refund outcome: `deposit_refund_apply` (0255, re-issued from 0242:848)

Ticket rows skip the court/reservation locks (0242:869-870) and `set_config` (0242:872). On
`succeeded`, instead of the `deposit_refunded` insert (0242:893-902), a ticket row with a push token and

B3) and audits `ticket.refunded`. Deposit rows are verbatim. The tickets are already `cashed_out`: a

`refund_failed` (Qi refused, or ten unanswered attempts, 0242:914-915): the row shows in

"Settled another way" (`deposit_refund_manual`, manager PIN, note says how: cash at the desk, transfer).
The tickets stay `cashed_out` in every case.

#### 3.7.4 DF-20: `app.ticket_refund_deleted(p_guest_id uuid default null) returns int` (0255, internal)

For each ticket purchase (`succeeded`) of a deleted guest (`profiles.deleted_at` set; only `p_guest_id`
when given) that has ≥ 1 `available` ticket and no `reserved`/`in_use` ticket, in id order, at most 50

Callers (DB lane, required):
- `delete_my_account` (0260) calls `app.ticket_refund_deleted(v_uid)` after it has left the filling
  matches and withdrawn the requests (their tickets are `available` by then).
- `match_sweep` (0259) calls `app.ticket_refund_deleted(null)` as its last step, in its own exception
  block. This refunds a purchase whose last ticket was locked in a booked match (DF-20's late leave)
  within 30 s of that ticket being released or forfeited. A forfeited ticket is not refunded.

A purchase still `created|pending` at deletion is caught at its SUCCESS (§3.5.3 step 4). No push (the
token is gone, 0077:190). Qi refunds to the card; no account is needed.

### 3.8 Seat money at the desk

#### 3.8.1 The engine: `app.match_money(p_match_id uuid, p_exclude_tab_id uuid) returns jsonb` (0256, internal)

`match_seat_money(uuid)` (§1.5) is a wrapper: `select … from jsonb_to_recordset(app.match_money($1,
null)->'seats')`. `court_fee_written_off(uuid, uuid)` reads the same engine with the excluded tab. One
engine, so the booking figures and the seat figures cannot drift.

Inputs, for the match's reservation `r` (only when `r.kind='booking'` and `r.status in
('confirmed','arrived','completed')`; otherwise every figure is 0 and `phase='not_live'`):
- `M = matches.price_iqd` (Σ shares, DF-3); `P = r.price_iqd` (moves with DF-4); `started = now() >=
  r.start_at`.
- `links(s)` = Σ `payment_match_seats.amount_iqd` of seat `s` over payments whose tab is `settled`, not
  merged away, and not `p_exclude_tab_id` (the filter of `court_fee_paid`, 0242:1533-1538). Gross:
  refunds are not netted, exactly as `court_fee_paid` does not net them (0106:120-124).
- `paid = app.court_fee_paid(r.id, p_exclude_tab_id)`.

The **carrier** of a `seat_no` is its seat with status `in`, `attended`, `no_show` or `left_late` that no
other such seat replaces (`replaces_seat_id`). A `seat_no` with no carrier is **vacant**. Seats with
status `left`, `removed`, `cancelled`, `refilled` carry nothing.

Raw figures per `seat_no`, share `S = shares_iqd[seat_no]`, `L = least(links(carrier), S)`:

| Carrier | Before start | After start |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `in` or `attended`, not written off | owed `S − L` | owed `S − L` |
| `in` or `attended`, `written_off_at` set (§3.8.5) | written off `S − L` (`manual`) | same |
| `no_show` | written off `S − L` (`no_show`) | same |
| `left_late` | open `S − L` | written off `S − L` (`left_late`) |
| vacant | open `S` | written off `S` (`vacant`) |

Then the credit pool (MD-9): `U = greatest(paid − Σ L over carriers, 0)` (money on the bill that no
carrying seat has: unlinked payments, and links on seats that no longer carry), `D = greatest(P − M, 0)`,
`C = greatest(M − P, 0)`, `K = U + C`. `K` pays `D` first (`delta_owed = D − that`), then owed seats from
the highest `seat_no` down (`credit_iqd`), then written-off seats from the highest `seat_no` down. What is
left is `over_iqd`. Open shares are never credited (the seat may still be refilled and must owe its share).

Output:

```json
{ "match_id", "reservation_id", "phase": "not_live|booked|started",
  "price_iqd": 40000, "booking_price_iqd": 40000, "price_delta_iqd": 0,
  "paid_iqd": 20000, "unassigned_iqd": 0, "delta_owed_iqd": 0,
  "owed_iqd": 10000, "written_off_iqd": 10000, "open_iqd": 0, "over_iqd": 0,
  "seats": [ { "seat_id": "uuid|null", "seat_no": 1, "kind": "account|friend|desk|vacant",
               "status": "attended", "carrying": true, "share_iqd": 10000,
               "paid_desk_iqd": 10000, "credit_iqd": 0, "owed_iqd": 0, "written_off_iqd": 0,
               "write_off": null | "manual|no_show|left_late|vacant", "open_iqd": 0 } ] }
```

`seats[]` holds one row per carrier or vacant `seat_no` (1..4), then one row per non-carrying seat
(`carrying:false`, only `paid_desk_iqd` can be non-zero). Identity: with `over_iqd = 0`,
`P − paid − written_off = owed + open + delta_owed` (invariant M3).

#### 3.8.3 `app.match_seat_settle(p_seat_ids uuid[], p_method payment_method, p_expected_owed_iqd bigint, p_tendered_iqd bigint default null, p_amount_iqd bigint default null, p_idempotency_key text default null, p_device_id text default null) → jsonb` (0258)

Cashier, court_desk, manager, owner at the match's branch (not shop_staff: the operator gates it on
`takeSeatPayment`, never `takeCourtPayment`, which includes shop_staff at `op/lib/auth.tsx:581`).
Online-only (DF-11). One call settles one fresh tab in full (MD-8).

Refusals, in order:
1. `FORBIDDEN`.
2. `INVALID_ARGUMENT`: `p_seat_ids` null, empty, more than 4 or with duplicates; `p_expected_owed_iqd`
   null or < 1; `p_amount_iqd` given and < 1.
3. `SEAT_NOT_FOUND`: an unknown seat, or seats of two matches.
4. `VENUE_MISMATCH`: not `is_staff_at` the match's branch; then `set_config('app.venue_id', …)`.
5. `app.claim_replay(p_idempotency_key, 'match_seat_settle')` (0049:68): a replay returns the stored
   result; `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`.
6. `MATCH_NOT_BOOKED`: the match is not `booked`/`played`, or its reservation is not live.
7. `NO_OPEN_DAY`: `app.current_open_day_locked()` (day_sessions first, 0106 order).
8. `BOOKING_TAB_OPEN` (detail = tab id): the booking has a live tab with orders, payments or adjustments.
   An **empty** live tab (which `cancel_tab` cannot remove, 0244:631-635) is adopted: locked `for update`
   and used as the fresh tab. With no live tab, `app.open_tab(p_reservation_id => r.id, p_device_id =>
   p_device_id)` (0244:70) opens one.
9. `NOTHING_OWED` (detail = seat id): a selected seat owes 0.
10. `SEAT_OWED_CHANGED` (detail `expected X, now Y`): Σ `owed_iqd` of the selected seats, **computed in a
    statement after step 8**, differs from `p_expected_owed_iqd`.
11. `INVALID_AMOUNT`: `p_amount_iqd` > owed.
12. From `settle_tab`: `TENDER_SHORT`, `TENDER_CARD`, `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`, and the till-shift stamp's own
    refusals (0205).

Then: `v_amount := coalesce(p_amount_iqd, owed)`; `update tabs set court_cap_iqd = v_amount`; if
`compute_tab_totals(tab).court_iqd <> v_amount` → `SEAT_OWED_CHANGED`; `app.settle_tab(tab, p_method,
p_tendered_iqd, v_amount, p_idempotency_key, p_device_id, v_amount)` (0244:312; `p_expected_total_iqd =
v_amount` makes `TOTAL_CHANGED` impossible after the check); insert `payment_match_seats` rows, spreading
`v_amount` over the seats in the order given, each up to its owed; audit `match.seat_settle`;
`finish_replay`.

Returns `{duplicate:false, payment_id, tab_id, amount_iqd, change_iqd, seats:[{seat_id, seat_no,
applied_iqd, owed_iqd}], booking_remaining_iqd}` (`owed_iqd` after the payment).

Why the tab step serialises two desks on one booking: `tabs_one_live_per_reservation` (0106:113-115)
makes the second insert wait for the first transaction; when it commits, the first tab is `settled`, so
the second insert goes through (or answers `BOOKING_TAB_OPEN`, retryable), and its owed check (step 10)
runs in a new statement that sees the first payment's links. A test drives this with two connections.

Partial payment: `p_amount_iqd` smaller than owed still settles its tab in full; the seat keeps owing
the rest.

#### 3.8.4 `app.match_link_payment(p_payment_id uuid, p_allocations jsonb, p_idempotency_key text default null) → jsonb` (0258)

Attributes a payment taken on the booking's normal bill (the offline path: queued `tab.open`/`tab.settle`

#### 3.8.7 DF-4, DF-16, call-off-short

- **DF-4.** A desk move or extend re-prices the reservation (0150:133-159, 0071:500-524). Shares stay
  stamped. `price_delta_iqd = P − M`: a rise is `delta_owed_iqd`, paid on the normal bill; a fall is
  credit, applied to owed seats from the highest `seat_no` down.
- **DF-16.** Café orders by match players go on their own café tab. Enforced in the operator (MD-10):
  "Charge to booking" never lists a match booking, and the match bill offers no café actions. If a café
  line reaches a match booking anyway, seat figures are unaffected (links cap at the tab's `court_iqd`)
  and `match_link_payment` refuses that live tab (`tab_has_orders`) until it is settled normally.
- **Call-off-short (OM-47).** `desk_call_off_short` (DB) cancels the booking (`called_off_short`):
  `court_fee_remaining` and `court_fee_written_off` are 0 because the booking is not live (0106:145-146);
  nobody owes anything. Tickets: present players released, the no-show forfeited (DB). Desk money
  already taken shows as `court_refund_due_iqd` on the bill (0242:1631-1633) and a manager refunds it at
  the till (`app.refund`, PIN). A staff cancel of a booked match is the same with every ticket released.

### 3.13 Money invariants (the tests assert them; `assertMatchMoney(matchId)` and `assertTicketLedger(guestId)` run after every case)

1. `Σ matches.shares_iqd = matches.price_iqd`; each seat's `share_iqd = shares_iqd[seat_no]`; the shares
   match core `splitEvenly` (`packages/core/src/money/split.ts:16`) for the same price.
2. The booked reservation is created with `price_iqd = matches.price_iqd`.
3. For a live match booking with `over_iqd = 0`: `court_fee_paid + court_fee_written_off +
   court_fee_remaining = price_iqd`, and `court_fee_remaining = owed + open + delta_owed` (§3.8.1).
4. Per carrying seat: `paid_desk (≤ share) + credit + owed + written_off + open = share_iqd`.
5. After any `match_seat_settle` or `match_link_payment` commit, the booking has no live tab. Every tab
   with `court_cap_iqd` set is `settled` with `court_iqd = court_cap_iqd`; for a `match_seat_settle` tab
   also `total_iqd = court_iqd = Σ its payment_match_seats.amount_iqd`.
6. Per payment, Σ links ≤ `amount_iqd −` its refunds at link time; per tab, Σ links ≤ `court_iqd`; per
   seat, Σ links ≤ `share_iqd`.
7. A not-live match booking (called off, cancelled, all no-show): `court_fee_remaining = 0` and
   `court_fee_written_off = 0`.
8. No ticket row is ever counted by `deposit_net_paid`, `court_fee_paid`, `my_reservations`,
   `booking_bill` or cash/card.
9. A booking with no match: `court_fee_remaining`, `compute_tab_totals`, `booking_bill` (old keys) and
   `booking_bill_states` (old keys) equal their 0106/0242 values. `deposits.test.ts` and
   `desk-payment.test.ts` pass unmodified.
10. Sandbox: no sandbox match has a `reservation_id`; sandbox rows and tickets add 0 to every §3.10
    figure.

Ticket ledger:

11. A ticket purchase row in `succeeded|refund_*|refunded` whose `refund_reason` is not
    `amount_mismatch` has exactly `ticket_count` tickets; any other purchase row has none.
12. Each ticket: `price_iqd = purchase.quoted_price_iqd`, `sandbox = purchase.sandbox`; each purchase:
    `amount_iqd = ticket_count × quoted_price_iqd`.
13. A purchase with a `cashed_out` ticket is `refund_pending|refund_failed|refunded` with reason
    `ticket_cashout` or `account_deleted`, `refund_amount_iqd = Σ price_iqd` of its `cashed_out` tickets,
    and none of its tickets is `available`, `reserved` or `in_use`.
14. A purchase row is refunded at most once (0241:56-57, 73-74) and never before it succeeded.
15. `reserved ⇔ request_id`, `in_use ⇔ seat_id` (§1.2); an `in_use` ticket's seat is `in` or `left_late`
    and belongs to its owner (the holder for a friend seat); a `reserved` ticket's request is `pending`.
16. A ticket's latest event matches its status (`bought`/`released`/`restored` ↔ `available`, `reserved`,
    `locked` ↔ `in_use`, `forfeited`, `cashed_out`).
17. `ticketSales = liability + forfeited + cashed out` over all time, per currency of record (non-sandbox).
18. An `in_use` or `reserved` ticket has `sandbox = matches.sandbox` of its match.

### Conflicts with §1

1. **`match_seat_write_off` cannot be PIN-gated with the §1.7 signature.** The operator proves the PIN
   only when the arguments carry `p_pin` and then passes every argument to the RPC
   (`op/lib/appRpc.ts:69-79`); the RPC spends the grant with `consume_pin_grant(p_device_id)`
   (0156:446). Without `p_pin` the PIN is never proved (`PIN_GRANT_REQUIRED` for ever); with it and no
   such parameter PostgREST answers PGRST202. Fix: `match_seat_write_off(p_seat_id uuid, p_reason text,
   p_pin text, p_device_id text default null)`, the `deposit_refund_manual` shape (0242:1208-1213).
2. **`tabs.court_cap_iqd` "set only by `match_seat_settle` on a fresh tab" (§1.2).** An empty live tab
   on the booking can neither be settled nor cancelled while court is owed (0244:631-635), and a partly
   paid normal bill must be closable for day close. Fix the wording to: "set by `match_seat_settle` on the
   tab it settles (fresh, or an adopted empty live tab) and by `match_link_payment` when it closes a live
   court-only tab at what was paid on it."
3. **§1.9 payload `{route:'match', id, …}` has no match for `tickets_refunded`.** Fix: `route` is
   `'match'` or `'tickets'`; `tickets_refunded` uses `{route:'tickets', id:null, title_key:

4. **§1.4's walker list** names `match_sweep`, `deposit_apply`, `ticket_settle_success`. Add

### Open technical questions

1. **Qi fees.** A 10,000 IQD ticket bought and later cashed out costs a payment fee and maybe a refund
   fee; DF-21 returns the full price. Who bears them, and is there a Qi minimum amount? (ASK QI, with the
   deposit questions, `qi-deposit-plan-2026-09-20.md:111,114`.)
2. **Qi refund time limit.** A cash-out or DF-20 refund months after the purchase may exceed Qi's
   window; it would land in `refund_failed` and be settled by hand ("Settled another way").
3. **Headline revenue.** `padelRevenue` stays booked, so written-off shares are in it while
   `ticketForfeits` are not. Keep the headline and show both lines beside it (this section's default), or
   net them in? Owner's call.
4. **Write-off undo.** A walked-out player who comes back to pay cannot be taken through
   `match_seat_settle` (the seat owes 0). Add a manager "undo write-off", or let `match_seat_settle`
   clear a `manual` write-off?
5. **Cash handed back by "Settled another way"** leaves the drawer without a till movement (the 0242
   behaviour for deposits). Acceptable for tickets too?
6. **The wallet limit (MD-6)** and **the 900 s window (MD-7)** are constants, not settings.
7. **Pool order (MD-9).** Unassigned money credits the highest `seat_no` first. The desk can always make
   it exact with `match_link_payment`; is a fixed order acceptable on the bill?
8. **Tax on seat money.** Court money is untaxed today (0106:215-271); if the client taxes match seats,
   the court line needs a tax path in `compute_tab_totals`.
9. **Liability as of a past date** (`ticketLiability`) reads current statuses, so a forfeit restored
   after the period counts as live at the period end. Exact history would replay `match_ticket_events`.
