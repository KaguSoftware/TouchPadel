# Multi-venue audit and fixes (2026-09-26)

The same day slices 2–4 went live (0207–0227), Parsa asked for a check of the whole multi-venue
system. Three read-only audits covered the venue resolver and branch lifecycle, every
venue-scoped RPC, policy and report, and the operator, mobile, web and edge code. The top findings
were verified against the code before any fix. This note records what was wrong, what changed
(migrations **0228–0235** plus the apps), and Parsa's three calls.

**None of this was visible in production.** Every finding needs a second open branch. With one
branch, `resolve_venue` always falls through to "the only open venue", and the guards never
disagree. The fixes had to land before the first "Open a new branch".

## Parsa's calls

| # | Question | Call |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | What does a machine that is not a registered station do? | **It registers on purpose only.** A heartbeat never registers or revives a station; an unregistered machine's beat is a no-op that says so (`registered: false`). Tills are registered in Settings › Venue details › Stations. Existing tills stay registered. The owner retires their own office PC there once, if it was auto-registered before 0229. |
| A2 | How does "Close branch" behave? | **Refuse, tidy up, keep history.** It is refused while bookings, holds or active series are still to come (`BRANCH_HAS_BOOKINGS`). Otherwise it ends live guest sessions, retires the branch's stations and ends its degraded period. The owner can still read a closed branch's reports. |
| A3 | How does it ship? | One batch: migrations 0228–0235 and the app fixes, pushed together after every gate. The operator tag stays Parsa's to cut. |

## What was wrong

Two findings blocked the owner's multi-branch flow outright:

- **Every machine became a station.** The heartbeat auto-registered any id, including the owner's
  PC, at whatever branch resolved. A registered machine never shows the switcher, and retiring it
  did not stick: the next beat revived it.
- **Reads and writes ranked the two headers in opposite orders.**
  - `visible_venue_ids` read `x-venue-scope` first; `resolve_venue` read `x-station-id` first.
  - So a screen could show branch B while every default-based write went to A. That includes new
    courts and menu items, shop tabs, `close_day` and deliveries.

The rest, grouped:

- **Silent swaps.**
  - A body that asserted a closed branch (`app.venue_id`) fell through to the headers, the caller's
    membership or "the only open branch".
  - Examples: a refund of a closed branch's payment was filed on another branch's day, and a guest
    still seated at a closed branch ordered into another branch's kitchen.
  - Staff whose only branch closed kept writing at the only open one.
- **No branch check on writes.**
  - About seventy staff RPCs updated or deleted rows with a role check only: `set_recipe`,
    `upsert_court`, `delete_court`, `set_item_*`, the modifier RPCs, `mark_reservation`,
    `cancel_reservation`, `move_reservation`, `confirm_booking` and others.
  - Nothing stopped a row pointing at another branch's row. A guest order could carry B's items:
    priced at B, stock taken from B's batches, filed at A.
- **Reads across branches.**
  - `table_qr_tokens` printed every branch's QR codes, with duplicate table numbers.
  - `audit_log_page` showed every branch's audit trail.
  - `kitchen_board` ignored the scope.
  - `menu_availability` covered every branch.
  - Six money and stock child tables had role-only policies.
- **Two cross-branch trust gaps:**
  - Any branch's manager PIN approved money actions at every branch.
  - A staffer at one branch could beat another branch's till: fake its liveness, take its
    `staff_id`, or set its queue depth.
- **Telegram:** a "void" tap failed with two open branches (`VENUE_REQUIRED` in a service-role call),
  the handler re-raised it (a webhook retry storm), and its rows were filed at the default branch.
- **Branch lifecycle.**
  - Two concurrent `close_branch` calls could both pass `LAST_OPEN_BRANCH`.
  - `open_day` could commit just after `BRANCH_DAY_OPEN`.
  - Nothing was tidied on close.
  - `create_branch` could fail on a foreign tax group, and copied the deprecated `llm_*` columns.
  - Readiness counted expired rate rules.
- **Operator.**
  - Screens loaded before the branch was known and were never reset.
  - A Preparing branch's hours could not be read (the public view lists open branches only).
  - Editors kept a draft from the last branch and saved it into the new one.
  - Queued offline writes did not say which branch they were for.
  - "All branches" leaked into badges and cached lists.
  - The branch list was not tied to the signed-in person.
  - The assistant and Telegram Diagnose read the wrong branch.
- **Mobile and web.**
  - Mobile: a quick branch switch back landed on a realtime channel still leaving; booking detail
    guessed the branch from active courts.
  - Web: a 12 h table cookie overrode `?b=`; a failed branch-list read was cached for a minute and
    replaced by every branch's menu at once; a new branch without an address showed the original
    club's in the site and its JSON-LD.
- **Performance:** `app.is_staff(...)` and `app.staff_role()` ran once per row in almost every
  policy, and every heartbeat swept every branch twice.

## Server, by migration

| # | Migration | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 0228 | `resolver_hardening` | `resolve_venue` never swaps. A closed station, a closed or unknown `app.venue_id`, or a scope the caller may not use each answer NULL. Staff with no open branch skip step 4. The station header outranks the scope in both `resolve_venue` and `visible_venue_ids`. Headers count for staff only. `readable_venue_ids` lets the owner read a closed branch (scope header, "All branches"). `all:<uuid>` names the clock "All branches" uses (`analysis_venue`). `is_staff_at` is false for a closed branch, owner included. `default_venue` never returns NULL while a branch exists. anon loses `current_venue` and `current_venue_or_default`. |
| 0229 | `stations_on_purpose` | A1. `heartbeat` works only on a live station, for its own branch's staff, sweeping its own branch; anything else is a `registered: false` no-op. The heartbeat trigger never registers or revives. `register_station` takes an advisory lock on the id, clears a moved station's rota (`STATION_HAS_HISTORY` once it has till shifts), and makes a desk or kitchen screen stop counting as a till. `retire_station` clears the rota. `set_station_staff` works inside one branch. The cron sweep ends a period left at a branch that is no longer open. |
| 0230 | `branch_write_guard` | `app.trg_branch_guard`, the `zz_branch_guard` trigger on every branch table and child table (generated from the live foreign keys). (1) Every link names a row of the same branch, for every writer. (2) A staff writer must work at the row's branch, unless a definer body asserted it or it is their own guest row. Refusals raise `VENUE_MISMATCH`. This closes the missing checks on about seventy RPCs, and on the next new one, in one place. |
| 0231 | `branch_scoped_reads` | Scoped to the branch in scope: `preview_series` / `create_series` alternatives, `series_detail`, `booking_bill`, `table_qr_tokens` (now naming the branch), `generate_table_token`, `audit_log_page`, `kitchen_board` and `menu_availability` (each item's own business day). `verify_manager_pin` matches the acting branch's managers (and the owner). `unpaid_played_bookings` is bounded. Fixed `report_cafe`'s and/or precedence and `receive_delivery`'s supplier check. Eight child read policies go through the parent's branch. |
| 0232 | `telegram_branch` | `telegram_apply_action` asserts the order's or call's branch before writing, and turns a branch refusal into a `refused` toast. |
| 0233 | `branch_lifecycle` | `open_branch`, `close_branch` and `open_day` share one lock. `open_day` refuses a closed branch (`VENUE_CLOSED`). `close_branch` refuses bookings still to come (`BRANCH_HAS_BOOKINGS`), then tidies up and audits while the branch is still open. `create_branch` takes one chain-wide lock, has a tax-group fallback and no longer copies the deprecated columns. Readiness counts only rules valid today. `register_staff` and `set_staff_venues` refuse a closed branch. |
| 0234 | `policy_initplans` | 103 policies re-created from their live definitions, with `app.is_staff(...)` / `app.staff_role()` wrapped in `(select …)`: one evaluation per statement. |
| 0235 | `my_reservations_venue` | `my_reservations` returns the booking's `venue_id`. |

New error codes (operator, EN + AR): `STATION_RETIRED`, `STATION_HAS_HISTORY`,
`BRANCH_HAS_BOOKINGS`, `VENUE_CLOSED`.

## Apps and edge functions

**Operator:**
- The station header is set at module load. The first requests of a boot carry the remembered
  branch, and the cache resets when the resolved branch differs.
- The branch lists are keyed by user.
- The routed screen remounts on a branch change.
- `fetchVenueSettings` reads the RLS-scoped `venue_settings` table.
- "All branches" rides only on report, analytics and panel RPCs (`REPORT_RPC`), and a report can
  pick a closed branch.
- Queued writes carry `venueScope` (shell queue v6).
- Setup › Branches:
  - "Fix" and "Print QR" are disabled on another branch's station.
  - A closed branch shows no "Open to guests".
- Diagnose and the assistant send the branch in the body. The edge functions do no CORS of their
  own, so no browser-side header is added to edge calls.

**Edge functions:**
- `replay` forwards `venue_scope`.
- `assistant-chat` forwards the scope server to server and reads that branch's business-day hour.
- `staff-admin` and `telegram-diagnose` return 400 on a malformed `venue_id`.

**Mobile:**
- The courts and floor channels wait for a leaving channel before re-joining.
- Booking detail uses the row's branch.
- Transient booking state resets on a branch change, and the duration clamps to what the branch
  offers.

**Web:**
- `?b=` beats a table cookie; that table then only browses.
- A failed read is never cached.
- An unreadable branch list shows the error state.
- Landing categories are deduplicated.
- A later branch without an address shows none.

## Tests and gates

- `tests/multi-venue-audit.test.ts` (11 cases, two open branches, rolled-back psql for closed
  branches) covers:
  - headers agreeing;
  - refused scopes;
  - closed-branch writes;
  - orphaned staff;
  - cross-branch links;
  - cross-branch writes;
  - the QR sheet;
  - PIN scope;
  - reading a closed branch;
  - `BRANCH_HAS_BOOKINGS` and `VENUE_CLOSED`;
  - heartbeats.
- Updated pins:
  - `multi-venue` cases 10 and 14 (no auto-registration; the cron sweeps every branch);
  - the RLS matrix (anon `current_venue*`);
  - `hiring` (the only trigger on candidates is the guard).
- Fixtures that built a "far" branch linked to the home branch's tax group or ingredients now give
  it its own.
- Heartbeat tests register their stations (`registerTestStation`), and the dev seed registers
  `DEV1`.
- Harnesses that write fixtures as postgres clear `request.jwt.claims`.
- Web menu tests, the shell's queue v6 and envelope, and the operator's scope-header test follow
  the new rules.

## Rollout

1. Push (one batch). Migrations 0228–0235 reach hosted through `db-migrate.yml`; `replay`,
   `assistant-chat`, `staff-admin` and `telegram-diagnose` redeploy through `functions-deploy.yml`.
   With one open branch, users see no change, apart from:
   - the heartbeat no longer registering new machines;
   - Settings › Stations being the way to register a till.
2. Cut the operator tag and install it on every station (it already had to go out before a second
   branch; it now carries these fixes too).
3. On the owner's office PC, open Settings › Venue details › Stations and retire it if a pre-0229
   heartbeat registered it. The switcher then appears once a second branch exists.
4. Mobile rides the 1.0 store build.

## Still open

- **Drop the legacy topics and columns:** the legacy `kds` / `floor` / `courts` topics still carry
  every branch until they are dropped (after the operator tag). The deprecated
  `venue_settings.llm_*` columns wait for the same migration.
- **Two-branch e2e:** a two-branch Playwright spec is still to write.
- **Telegram allowlist:** `telegram_staff` is chain-wide. An allowlisted person in one branch's group
  can act on that branch's orders (by design, recorded here).
